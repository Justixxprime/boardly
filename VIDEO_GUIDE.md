# What to actually film

The single biggest gap on this site right now is proof. Every technical claim is real, but nobody watching can see it work with their own eyes yet. This is the highest leverage thing you can do, higher than any more design work.

You do not need professional equipment. Screen recording software (QuickTime on Mac, the built in Xbox Game Bar on Windows, or a free tool like OBS) and your own voice, or no voice at all, is enough. Fifteen to thirty seconds per clip. Nobody needs a minute.

## Boardly, the marketplace payment flow

This is your single most valuable clip, because it proves the hardest technical claim on the whole site.

Record: opening the marketplace, booking a service as a client, and watching the booking move from pending to confirmed once payment goes through. If you have a test payment mode, use it. Show the payout side too if you can, a provider seeing the booking appear and the payout eventually release.

Why this one matters most: the case study says a webhook confirms payment before anything is marked paid. A recording proves that sequence actually happens, instead of asking someone to trust a paragraph.

## Boardly, the AI board assistant

Record: typing a question into the assistant that requires it to search across tasks by meaning, not just an exact keyword match, and watching it return the right task. For example, ask about something described differently than how it was originally titled.

Why this one matters: it is the single most impressive sounding claim on the site, and the easiest for a skeptical reader to assume is exaggerated.

## Victorious Concept, the real checkout

Record: adding a product to cart, going through checkout, and reaching a real Paystack payment screen. You do not need to complete a real payment for this one, showing the real gateway loading with real product and price data already proves the integration is genuine, not mocked.

If you can safely show a completed order appearing in the admin panel afterward, that second half is worth its own fifteen seconds.

## First Experts Logistics, the tracking page

Record: entering a real tracking number and watching the page correctly identify the carrier and pull a live status. This is a quieter, less flashy clip than the payment ones, but it is concrete and easy to follow even for someone who does not code.

## Fernhollow, one of the ten no paid API features

Record: the sunrise and sunset countdown actually updating, or the ambient soundscape playing (with sound on for this one clip only). Pick whichever one is easiest to capture clearly. This is the clip that proves you can build something that feels impressive without leaning on someone else's paid service.

## What NOT to film

Do not record generic scrolling through a homepage or a static page with no interaction. That is not proof of anything, it is just a slower way to look at a screenshot. Every clip should show something happening as a direct result of something you clicked, typed, or triggered.

Do not narrate with marketing language over the recording if you add voice. "And here we can see the payment confirming in real time" is fine. "This seamless, cutting edge experience" is not, for the same reason that language is banned from the site's written copy.

## Where these go once you have them

Short term: even one of these, uploaded anywhere public (X, LinkedIn, a personal video link) and linked from the relevant case study, does more for credibility than a week of further design work here.
