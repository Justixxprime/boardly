FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is my data private?

Yes, every row is tied to your workspace and locked with database-level row security, so no other user can ever read or edit it.

How does getting paid actually work?

You send an invoice or a payment link, your client pays through the provider, and Boardly verifies the payment server-side before marking anything paid. Marketplace jobs work the same way, with payment held until you confirm the deliverable.

Is the marketplace optional?

Yes. You can run Boardly purely for your own work and clients and never publish a marketplace profile.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is required to start. Boardly also works as an installable app on your phone or desktop if you want it on your home screen, and it keeps working with no connection.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, the whole interface, including boards and drag-and-drop, is built to work down to a small phone screen.
